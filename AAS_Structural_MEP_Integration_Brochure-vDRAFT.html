Product 04 / RK-INT

Integration that lands
with the steel.

BIM · Utility envelopes
Equipment interfaces
MEP eng optional · Handoff

RK-INT is the coordination layer on top of RK-MAS, RK-MEP, and RK-CUDR. We engineer the structural support and package the interfaces so EPC, trades, OEMs, and controls teams share one geometry, one sequence, and one punchable handoff. When required, full MEP engineering slots into the same flow — design packages and coordinated models before fabrication.

Diagram · 6-step integration flow (MEP eng optional) · texture from original brochure

01 · BIM multi-trade

IFC / Revit / BIM360 exchange; clash awareness for tray, pipe, busway, and structure; models aligned to erection sequence.

02 · Utility envelopes

Corridor & platform geometry vs facility utilities — electrical paths, compressed air, exhaust, gas, HVAC / MUA — with owner MEP EOR or AAS MEP engineering.

03 · MEP eng (optional)

Full mechanical, electrical, plumbing / process utilities design packages & coordinated models when AAS is engaged for MEP engineering. Stamp jurisdiction TBD per project.

04 · Equipment interfaces

Reactions, vibration, access, and conveyance clearances packaged for OEM and EPC use.

05 · Commissioning handoff

Install sequence, utilities punch lists, FAT/SAT mindset packages — conceptual coordination; licensed PLC/HMI or safety-circuit EOR remains with designated controls authority unless separately contracted.

06 · Sequenced delivery

Shop packages and ship sets tied to crane plans — factory-direct visibility from design through outbound.

Default vs available: Default = structural support + integration coordination with EPC/MEP partners. Available when required = full MEP engineering (M/E/P / process utilities) as AAS offered scope — design packages, coordinated models, stamped where applicable (PE jurisdiction TBD). Controls / safety-circuit EOR remains separately designated unless contracted.
