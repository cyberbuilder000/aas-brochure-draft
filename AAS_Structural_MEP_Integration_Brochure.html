Equipment interfaces
MEP engineering · Commissioning

RK-INT is the coordination layer on top of RK-MAS, RK-MEP, and RK-CUDR. We engineer the structural support and package the interfaces so EPC, trades, OEMs, and controls teams share one geometry, one sequence, and one punchable commissioning handoff. Full MEP engineering sits in the same flow as a peer capability — design packages and coordinated models before fabrication.

01 · BIM multi-trade

Model exchange via IFC / Revit / BIM 360; clash awareness for tray, pipe, busway, and structure; models aligned to erection sequence.

02 · Utility envelopes

Corridor & platform geometry vs facility utilities — electrical paths, compressed air, exhaust, gas, HVAC / MUA — with owner MEP EOR or AAS MEP engineering.

03 · MEP engineering

Full mechanical, electrical, plumbing / process utilities — design packages & coordinated models. Stamped drawings per project jurisdiction where applicable.

04 · Equipment interfaces

Reactions, vibration, access, and conveyance clearances packaged for OEM and EPC use.

05 · Commissioning handoff

Install sequence, utilities punch lists, FAT/SAT mindset packages — readiness documentation; licensed PLC/HMI or safety-circuit EOR remains with designated controls authority unless separately contracted.

06 · Sequenced delivery

Shop packages and ship sets tied to crane plans — factory-direct visibility from design through outbound.

Peer capabilities: Structural support, MEP engineering (M/E/P / process utilities), multi-trade integration, and commissioning readiness packages — coordinated with EPC partners. Controls / safety-circuit EOR remains separately designated unless contracted.
