**Product line**  
PRODUCT 04 / RK-INT  
*Allied Advanced Systems — Integration & Plant MEP Coordination*

**Headline**  
Integration that lands  
with the steel.

**Body**  
RK-INT is the coordination layer that sits on top of RK-MAS, RK-MEP, and RK-CUDR. We engineer the structural support **and** package the interfaces so EPC, trades, OEMs, and controls teams share one geometry, one sequence, and one punchable handoff. **When required, full MEP engineering slots into the same flow** — design packages and coordinated models before fabrication.

**Visual (HTML)**  
`assets/rk-int-flow.svg` — 6-step flow: Routing → BIM → MEP eng (optional) → Fabricate → Sequenced ship → Commissioning handoff (diagram).

**What’s included (brochure-safe scope)**

1. **BIM multi-trade coordination**  
   IFC / Revit / BIM360 exchange; clash awareness for tray, pipe, busway, and structure; model packages aligned to erection sequence.

2. **Utility load envelopes**  
   Corridor and platform geometry planned against facility utility needs — electrical (e.g. 480V paths), compressed air, exhaust, gas, HVAC / MUA envelopes — with owner MEP EOR **or** AAS MEP engineering when engaged.

3. **MEP engineering (optional / when required)**  
   Full mechanical, electrical, plumbing / process utilities — design packages, coordinated models; stamped where applicable (**PE stamp jurisdiction TBD** per project).

4. **Process equipment interfaces**  
   Reactions, vibration, access, and conveyance clearances packaged for OEM and EPC use; platforms and racks that respect process loads.

5. **Controls & commissioning handoff packages**  
   Install-sequence documentation, utilities punch lists, and FAT/SAT *mindset* packages — conceptual coordination and documentation support; licensed PLC/HMI or safety-circuit design of record remains with designated controls authority unless separately contracted.

6. **Sequenced delivery**  
   Shop packages and ship sets tied to crane plans and field erection — factory-direct visibility from design through outbound.

**Honest boundary box (print this)**  
> **Default:** AAS engineers structural support and provides multi-trade integration coordination with EPC/MEP partners.  
> **Available when required:** Full MEP engineering (M/E/P / process utilities) as AAS offered scope — design packages, coordinated models, stamped where applicable (PE jurisdiction TBD). Controls / safety-circuit EOR remains separately designated unless contracted.
