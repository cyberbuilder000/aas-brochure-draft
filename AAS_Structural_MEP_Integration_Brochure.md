# PAGE 07 — PRODUCT 04 / RK-INT

**Product line**  
PRODUCT 04 / RK-INT

**Headline**  
Integration that lands with the steel.

**Body**  
RK-INT is the coordination layer on top of RK-MAS, RK-MEP, and RK-CUDR. We engineer the structural support **and** package the interfaces so EPC, trades, OEMs, and controls teams share one geometry, one sequence, and one punchable commissioning handoff. Full MEP engineering sits in the same flow as a peer capability — design packages and coordinated models before fabrication.

**Visual**  
`assets/rk-int-flow.svg` — Routing → BIM → MEP eng → Fabricate → Ship → Commission

1. **BIM multi-trade** — Model exchange via IFC / Revit / BIM 360; clash awareness; models aligned to erection sequence.  
2. **Utility envelopes** — Corridor & platform geometry vs facility utilities — with owner MEP EOR or AAS MEP engineering.  
3. **MEP engineering** — Full M/E/P / process utilities — design packages & coordinated models; stamped drawings per jurisdiction where applicable.  
4. **Equipment interfaces** — Reactions, vibration, access, conveyance clearances for OEM and EPC.  
5. **Commissioning handoff** — Install sequence, utilities punch lists, FAT/SAT mindset packages — readiness documentation; licensed PLC/HMI or safety-circuit EOR remains designated unless separately contracted.  
6. **Sequenced delivery** — Shop packages and ship sets tied to crane plans.

**Peer capabilities note**  
Structural support, MEP engineering, multi-trade integration, and commissioning readiness packages — coordinated with EPC partners. Controls / safety-circuit EOR remains separately designated unless contracted.
